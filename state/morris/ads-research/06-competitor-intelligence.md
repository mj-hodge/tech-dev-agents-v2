## Strategic Observations

**Consolidation trend:** Sellics merged into Perpetua. Downstream AI appears inactive. The competitive field is narrowing at the top.

**Pricing transparency divide:** Teikametrics and SellerApp publish full pricing. Most enterprise players (Pacvue, Skai, Quartile, Intentwise) gate pricing behind demos. Perpetua is in the middle — publishes base prices but not % tiers.

**AI positioning convergence:** Every major platform claims AI/ML. Real differentiators are:
- *Retail-aware signals* (Intentwise, Skai): inventory, Buy Box, pricing fed into bid logic
- *Incrementality measurement* (Perpetua): measuring whether ads actually drove incremental sales
- *GenAI for content* (Teikametrics): applying AI beyond bidding to listing optimization
- *Commerce observability* (Intentwise, Skai): unified platform beyond just ad management
- *TACOS/organic-aware bidding* (m19): avoiding overbidding when organic rank is strong

**Channel breadth gap:** Pacvue and Skai cover 100+ retail networks. Most others are Amazon-primary. Multi-channel is a clear enterprise differentiator.

**SMB price floor:** Teikametrics ($149/mo), SellerApp ($99/mo), and Ad Badger represent the lowest accessible entry points. Perpetua's $695/mo floor is high for true SMB.

**Human services as differentiator:** Quartile and Teikametrics (Managed Services) combine software with human strategists — a hedge against pure AI commoditization.

**Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC):** Skai, Intentwise, Quartile, Teikametrics, and Perpetua all highlight AMC integration as a premium capability — audience segmentation, incrementality, and attribution beyond standard campaign data.
